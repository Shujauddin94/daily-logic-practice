Factorial in Math

In math, a factorial is an operation that multiplies a whole number by all the positive whole numbers below it, down to 1.

Definition

The factorial of a number n is written as:

n!

and means:

n! = n × (n − 1) × (n − 2) × ... × 1

Examples

5!

5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120

3!

3! = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6

1!

1! = 1

0!

0! = 1

(This is a special rule in math.)

Important Rules

  • Factorials are defined only for whole numbers (0, 1, 2, 3, ...).
  • Factorials grow very fast as numbers get bigger.
  • Negative numbers do not have factorials.
