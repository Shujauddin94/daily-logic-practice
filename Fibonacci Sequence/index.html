Fibonacci Sequence

The Fibonacci sequence is a series of numbers where each number is the sum of the two preceding numbers.

Mathematical Definition

F(0) = 0
F(1) = 1
F(n) = F(n − 1) + F(n − 2), for n ≥ 2

Example

0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13

Explanation

The first two numbers are 0 and 1. Every new number is calculated by adding the previous two numbers.
